You are responsible for compliance with any law regulating such uses, including impact-assessment, registration, and transparency obligations.

1. **Your Agent, your actions.** Actions taken by an Agent within the scope of the instructions, permissions, credentials, or funds you give it are your actions for all purposes under the Agreement, whether or not you reviewed each individual action, and whether or not they match what you intended. 2. **Monitoring.** You will supervise Agents proportionately to the harm they could cause, and will not leave an Agent running unattended with authority whose misuse you are not prepared to bear.
