## Important notice about on-chain data

**Please read this section carefully. It qualifies everything else in this Privacy Policy.**

The Service is built on the BSV blockchain, a public, decentralized network that we do not own, operate, or control.

- **Blockchain data is public and permanent.** Wallet addresses, transactions (including amounts, counterparty addresses, timestamps, and fees), and the full content of inscriptions are recorded on the blockchain, are publicly visible to anyone in the world, and are replicated across a decentralized network of independent nodes.
- **On-chain data cannot be deleted or changed.** Once recorded, blockchain data **cannot be deleted, modified, or anonymized by us or by anyone else**. This is a technical property of the blockchain, not a policy choice.
- **Our role is to read, not to control.** Our indexer and API read and organize data that is already public on the blockchain. Removing data from our index or gateway does not remove it from the blockchain, where it remains accessible through other tools.
- **Your privacy rights apply to off-chain data only.** Rights of deletion, erasure, correction, or rectification described in this Privacy Policy (including under state privacy laws and the GDPR) apply only to the **off-chain** data we control — such as account records, server logs, analytics data, and support records. They cannot be applied to data recorded on the blockchain.
- **Linking a wallet can identify you.** Blockchain addresses are pseudonymous, but if you connect a wallet to the Service, associate an address with an account, handle, or email, or otherwise publicly link an address to your identity, your on-chain activity (past and future) may become attributable to you by us and by third parties. Consider carefully what you inscribe and which addresses you link.
- **Think before you inscribe.** Any personal information you or others include in inscription content becomes permanently and publicly available on the blockchain. Do not inscribe personal information you may later want removed.

## Retention

We generally retain **off-chain** personal information (such as account data, logs, and support records) to fulfill the purposes for which we collected it, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements, establishing or defending legal claims, or for fraud prevention purposes. To determine the appropriate retention period, we may consider factors such as the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the personal information, the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure, the purposes for which we process it and whether we can achieve those purposes through other means, and the applicable legal requirements. When we no longer require the off-chain personal information we have collected about you, we may either delete it, anonymize it, or isolate it from further processing.

**On-chain data is different.** Data recorded on the blockchain — wallet addresses, transactions, and inscription content — is retained permanently by the blockchain network itself. We do not control that retention and cannot delete, modify, or anonymize on-chain data. Even if we remove on-chain data from our own index or gateway, it remains on the blockchain and available to others.
